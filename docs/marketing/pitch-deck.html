08 / 12

Differentiation

Batch Passport — ISO discipline by design.

↔

Forward & reverse trace

Any finished lot → shift, machine, operator, raw-material batch, QA result. Any complaint → batch → root cause.

ISO

Controlled documents

Logbook templates with doc/rev numbers, mandatory QA gates, calibration register, append-only audit events.

8D

Closed-loop CAPA

SLA clocks by severity. Complaints do not close until CAPA closes with root cause recorded.

Business rule: a roll may not be dispatched unless it passed QA and was put away as finished stock.
