# Product lifecycle: land → expand → platform

## Stages

| Stage | Buyer pain | Package | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Land** | Paper logbooks, weak traceability | Plant Start | Operators and QA live in MesaPlant; paper retired for pilot line |
| **Expand** | Quotes / inventory / finance disconnected | + MesaBook and/or MesaSell | MD sees commercial numbers linked with explicit link states |
| **Platform** | Vendors + executive reporting | Suite + Connect + MesaAnalytics | Multi-plant or audit-ready reporting |

Finance-first buyers may land on **Commercial Start**, then expand into MesaPlant.

## What ships with every land

- Organization Control (people, plants, access)
- Command for MD (exceptions from entitled modules)

## What never blocks land

- Full Manufacturing Suite
- Connect (needs MesaBook)
- MesaAnalytics

## Multi-plant

Header plant/company switchers scale without a new product. Entitlement can be per plant for MesaPlant and per company for MesaBook.
